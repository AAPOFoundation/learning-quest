# Animation pass (lq-anim) — decisions

Date: 2026-10-23. Branch `grok/lq-anim-20261023`. Scope is motion only: walk, characters, celebrations, transitions, and how much the canvas redraws. No new worlds, no save-format changes, no new assets.

## What was already there

The map already bobs the hero, waves flags, shimmers water, and throws a spark burst on a chime (`shared/fx2.js`). Buttons move 1px. Dialogue and the lesson overlay appear at once. A walk between hexes is a straight line at constant speed (0.14s). Low effects and `prefers-reduced-motion` turn the canvas effects off.

## Decisions

1. **One stride per hex, eased.** Position uses smoothstep so a step starts and stops softly. A hop (about 5.5px) is drawn by the figure, not baked into `hero.y`, so the camera does not bounce. Which foot leads flips each tile. The home-page wanderer uses the same step fields, one stride about every 42px.
2. **Characters stay procedural.** No sprite sheets. Idle is a small breath, a blink, and the old bob. Near the hero, a town person raises an arm. The old time-based leg swing remains only when `step` is missing, so older callers still walk.
3. **Chest lid is a short hinge.** It plays once when a chest changes from shut to open. A save that already has the chest open stays open. Low effects snaps the lid.
4. **Celebrations stay on the chime.** The same chime now carries a short floater: `+10 XP`, `+25 XP`, `+50 XP`, `Cleared`, `Well done`. Words are plain and not religious. Sparks are fewer, plus a ring, so the burst reads larger without more circles. Dust puffs land on each step, except the step that enters a town or leaves through a gate (that puff would be in the wrong scene).
5. **Scene change is a fade, not a slide.** Entering a place sets a dark veil for about 0.4s and still snaps the camera. A slide would show the wrong map. Bumping a blocked tile shakes the map for a moment and plays a soft thud, at most once every 0.32s.
6. **Quiz and code wins get a CSS beat.** A passed level and the final challenge use `panel cheer`. A Python challenge that passes every test uses `cq-out cheer`. Stars pop in one after another. The toast animation keeps `translateX(-50%)` so it stays centred. `prefers-reduced-motion` still removes CSS animation.
7. **Performance.** Static trees and rocks are stamped once and blitted (shared by shape: oak, pine, snow, bush, stone). Low effects, or reduced motion, and a hero who is standing still with no key, path, fog, or camera settle, redraw at about 8fps. A held key is never skipped. The tab being hidden skips the frame. Name plaques cache `measureText`. Particle and water drawing avoid `toFixed` in the inner loop.
8. **Low effects still means the quiet map.** Hop, veil, shake, dust, floaters, NPC waves, and the quest bob are off when Low effects is on or the reduced-motion query matches. The idle redraw cap applies only in that quiet mode.
9. **A tap shows the route.** Brass dots march from the hero to the chosen tile, with a ring on the last hex and a short ripple on the tap. Low effects keeps still dots and skips the march and the ripple. At most 48 dots.
10. **A turn is a sideways squash.** Changing facing plays it for about 0.14s. The town gate's door slides up when the hero is within two tiles, and snaps shut or open when motion is off.
11. **XP counts, then the bar follows.** The number eases to the new total over about half a second. The existing bar width transition is unchanged. Back on the world map, a region that just opened gets one burst and the word Open.
12. **Small UI beats.** The place name rises as it fades in. A dialogue portrait breathes. The pad shows a press. The quest card pulses when the next objective changes. Test rows arrive one after another. Each home-page world fills a thin bar to its cleared fraction. The vignette is painted once per window size, and the scene fade no longer builds a colour string each frame.
13. **Buildings are blitted in full effects.** Each kind is painted into one shared 2× sprite (about 280×320). Flags, smoke, wheels and flames redraw about 15 times a second. A still building is painted once. Fountains share a sprite, since they do not use the region colour. At most 48 sprites, and the least recently shown one is dropped. Low effects still draws live, and turning low effects on frees the sprites. A chest lid mid-swing and the town gate stay live so those stay smooth. Home-page name plaques cache their width. Objects outside the view are left out of the sort.
14. **A cleared town breathes.** Its name plate edge pulses gold while motion is on. Dialogue choices arrive one after another. The arrow that points to an off-screen objective pulses. The hero portrait and the party faces rise and fall by a pixel. Reduced motion removes those.
15. **Simulator bars ease in place.** Temperature, autocomplete and the context bench used to replace their markup on every slider tick, so a width transition never ran. Rows stay put and only the width changes, over 0.28s. The first paint grows from empty. Autocomplete rebuilds only when the word list changes, and a cut-off row fades. Reduced motion already turns transitions off.
16. **The camera keeps one pace.** Follow uses the time since the last frame. At 60 frames a second it matches the old step. The home-page wanderer does the same at its 30-frame cap. The mini-map picture still refreshes about six times a second. The hero dot and the gold ring draw every frame.
17. **A new question arrives in a short beat.** Choices ease in one after another, and the level bar grows from empty. Choosing an answer does not replay that entrance. Reduced motion already removes the animation.
18. **Lesson charts glide.** The learning-rate dots ease toward the new steps. The bowl is painted once. The threshold line slides, and a dot fades when it falls below the line. Reduced motion snaps both.
19. **Review cards match the quiz.** Choices arrive one after another. The reply and the next button rise after a pick. Every choice sinks a pixel while it is held. Reduced motion removes that.
20. **Quest marks keep one gold.** The cleared name edge and the quest ring used to build a new colour string each frame. They now set the opacity on one gold.
21. **Dialogue stays put between lines.** A new line from the same speaker no longer rebuilds the box, so it does not rise again. A different speaker still gets the short rise. Closing the box clears that memory, so the next talk rises once.
22. **Lists arrive in turn.** The pause menu, the quest log and the world-map rows each rise a little after the one above. The delay stops growing after the tenth row.
23. **The home path arrives in order.** The four world buttons, then the portfolio button, rise one after another. Reduced motion removes it.
24. **Skills bars grow, badges rise.** A skills bar starts empty and eases to the saved score. Badges rise without a fade, so a locked badge stays dim. The rise stops adding delay after the twelfth badge.
25. **A new companion fades in.** The first time the party is drawn, the faces you already have stay steady. A later join fades the newest face in. The fade is opacity only, so the breath can still move the face. Reduced motion removes it.
26. **The title actions rise.** Continue or Begin, then New journey when there is a save, then All games. The heading stays still. Reduced motion removes the rise.
27. **A cleared region portrait pops once.** The row of small portraits already brightens when a region is cleared. The one that just changed also pops. The first draw after a save only brightens, so old clears do not all pop at once.
28. **The talk button glows when it will do something.** On a touch pad, A pulses while a person, a hall, or a chest is on a neighbouring tile. It checks again only when the tile, the place, or the motion setting changes. Low effects and reduced motion leave it still.
29. **A still cape stirs.** While a figure stands, the cape hem shifts by about two pixels. Walking still uses the step drag, and the idle shift is off then. Low effects draws this with the rest of the quiet idle, at about 8 frames a second.
30. **A still staff stone pulses.** The pale stone on a staff grows and shrinks a little while the figure stands. It holds one size while the figure walks.
31. **Hair stirs while a figure stands.** The hairline shifts by about a pixel, on a different beat from the cape. Walking holds it still. Hoods and helms have no loose hair, so they stay put. Low effects draws this with the quiet idle, at about 8 frames a second.
32. **Mixed colours are remembered.** Robes, skin and walls mix the same pairs every frame. The rgb string is kept, so the hex parse runs once per pair. The picture does not change.
33. **A name fades in as you walk closer.** Inside about two tiles the name is solid. It fades across the next bit of distance and is gone by three tiles. Low effects and reduced motion keep the old hard edge.
34. **The draw list is reused.** Each frame used to build a new list of everything on screen, and a new empty save when there was none. The same slots are cleared and filled again. The quest arrow finds its town with a short loop. The picture does not change.
35. **The tile under the pointer lights up.** A soft hex fades in on that tile and fades out when the pointer leaves. It sits under people and buildings. Low effects and reduced motion show it at once, with no fade. A quiet map wakes for one frame so the light is not late.
36. **A still beard, plume and scholar board stir.** The beard tip swings about a pixel. The helm plume bobs. The flat scholar board tips a few degrees. Walking holds each one still. Low effects draws them with the quiet idle.
37. **The streak flame flickers.** The small fire by the day count rises and grows a little, on a loop. The number stays still. Reduced motion removes the flicker.
38. **One options bag for buildings.** Each building on screen used to get a new options object every frame. One bag is cleared and filled again. A lid, a light or a gate lift cannot leak onto the next building, because those fields are wiped first. The picture does not change.
39. **Next rises after an answer.** The quiz Next button, and the one after a Python challenge, rise just after the reply. The rise does not stay stuck on the button, so a press can still sink it. Reduced motion removes the rise.
40. **Party and satchel cards arrive in order.** The heading stays still. Each companion card, and each saved function, rises a little after the one above. The delay stops growing after the tenth card. An empty satchel rises once. Reduced motion removes it. The lists are built when the page opens, not on each keystroke.
41. **A nearby name is painted once.** Standing next to someone used to stroke the name every frame. The first time, it is drawn onto a small picture and then blitted, including while it fades. The words do not change.
42. **A new hint rises.** Only the hint that just opened rises. The ones already on screen stay put, because the list is rebuilt. Reduced motion removes the rise.
43. **A walk-through arrives step by step.** The plain-English plan lists its steps one after another. The heading stays still. The delay stops growing after the tenth step. Reduced motion removes it. Opening the plan again plays it once more.
44. **Dialogue appears at once when motion is off.** Low effects and reduced motion no longer reveal a line one letter at a time, and they skip the little ticks. The full line, the choices and the advance mark show on the first paint.
45. **A locked chest nudges its lid.** Trying a sealed chest lifts the lid a little, then it shuts, over about a quarter of a second. An open chest and a quiet setting do not nudge. The half-open lid stays live, so it is not baked into the building picture.
46. **Final-challenge choices arrive in turn.** Each choice rises a little after the one above, the same way a quiz choice does. The result screen is unchanged. Reduced motion removes the rise.
47. **A lesson page fades in.** Moving to the next page of a lesson fades the panel in. Only the opacity changes, so the page does not jump. Low effects and reduced motion show it at once. Other panels, including the word list, are left alone.
48. **A wrong choice settles sideways.** A correct choice still pops in place. A wrong one shifts a few pixels right, then left, then rests. The page itself does not shake. Reduced motion removes it.
49. **The code-challenge bar grows.** It used to appear at the right width, so the bar transition never ran. It now grows from empty to this question's place, the same way a quiz bar does. The first question is already empty, so it does not flash. Low effects and reduced motion keep the final width.
50. **The mini-map dot breathes.** The dot that marks you grows and shrinks a little. Low effects and reduced motion keep it one size. The picture behind it still refreshes about six times a second.
51. **Result buttons rise.** After a level, the re-read button rises, then Back to the map. After a final-challenge step, Continue rises. At the end, Replay rises, then Back to the map. The rise lets go, so a press can still sink the button. Reduced motion removes it.
52. **The home strip reuses its draw list.** Each frame used to build a new list of the four towns, the citadel and the wanderer. The same slots are filled again. The picture does not change.
53. **A failed test settles sideways.** A passing test still rises. A failing one shifts a few pixels, then rests, on the same beat as a wrong quiz choice. Reduced motion removes it.
54. **The way back rises.** Leave, Back, Got it and Back to the map rise when that page opens. The rise lets go, so a press can still sink the button. Reduced motion removes it. The menu rows already arrive on their own.
55. **The home wanderer kicks up dust.** Once each stride, a small puff lands at the feet, the same puff the map uses. A still frame and a quiet setting do not puff. The puff is drawn with the other motes, so it does not need its own loop.

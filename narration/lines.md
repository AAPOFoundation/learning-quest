## ai-evals

- **Meadow of First Marks** (`t0`): Welcome to the Meadow of First Marks. Here you learn what an eval is: a repeatable way to check how an AI system behaves. Start small, and look at the real outputs.
- **Rubric Wood** (`t1`): This is Rubric Wood. A good eval begins with clear cases and a written rubric that says what good looks like. Write it down before you run anything.
- **Judges' Hills** (`t2`): Welcome to the Judges' Hills. Graders can be exact matches, people, or another model acting as judge. Each has strengths and weaknesses, so compare a judge against human labels.
- **Dunes of Measure** (`t3`): You have reached the Dunes of Measure. One accuracy number can hide a lot. Look at precision, recall and how much graders agree, and remember that results are noisy.
- **Frost Reach** (`t4`): This is Frost Reach, the land of validity. Ask whether your eval measures what you care about. Keep some test cases aside that you never tune against.
- **Redfen Marsh** (`t5`): Welcome to Redfen Marsh. Here you probe on purpose for unreliable or harmful behaviour. When you find failures, rank them by how serious they are, not only by how many.
- **Highland of Synthesis** (`t6`): You stand in the Highland of Synthesis. A full eval suite ties a decision to behaviours, cases, graders, metrics and a report. Begin from the decision you need to make.
- **Citadel of the Eval Designer** (`k`): Welcome to the Citadel. The Launch Council will ask you to design an eval, step by step. Use everything the seven regions taught you.

## portfolio-guide

- **Harbour of Purpose** (`t0`): Welcome to the Harbour of Purpose. This game explains a plan for a portfolio that is mostly long positions. Hedges exist to soften drawdowns, and to make cash for later entries. This is education, not advice.
- **Cash Vault Plains** (`t1`): This is the Cash Vault. The plan keeps a fixed floor of cash that is never invested, plus a flexible layer that moves with market conditions. Written rules beat gut feel.
- **Trim Guild Hills** (`t2`): Welcome to the Trim Guild. Position caps stop one holding from sinking the whole book. Trim the amount above a cap, and stage it, rather than selling everything at once.
- **Hedge Smithy Dunes** (`t3`): You have found the Hedge Smithy. A put spread pays a limited amount if a price falls, and you can only lose what you paid. Try the calculator to see the numbers.
- **Basis Frost** (`t4`): This is Basis Frost. A hedge on one stock only helps if your holdings move with it. Size a hedge to a payout target, then check what it really tracks.
- **Signal Tower Heights** (`t5`): Welcome to the Signal Tower. Price action, R S I and M A C D can drive written rules. Ask for two of the three to agree, and act in steps.
- **Patience Highland** (`t6`): You have reached the Patience Highland. Income ideas like cash-secured puts and covered calls come later, and only with the cash set aside for them. Patience is part of the plan.
- **Keep of the Risk-Off Scenario** (`k`): Welcome to the Keep. The Storm Marshal will test you with a falling market. Use the cash floor, the caps, the hedge and patience.
